# Preserve operational execution causes and release clean failed checkouts

**Date** 2026-11-23 10:27 MSK

**Files** `web/lib/agents/create-failure.ts`, `web/lib/agents/finalization.ts`, `web/lib/runs/terminal-cause.ts`, `web/components/runs/operational-run-result.tsx`, `web/lib/gc/operational-disposition.ts`, EN/RU catalogs and focused tests

**Severity** medium

## Problem

A failed operational run could show `operational_execution_failed` together with a result-validation warning even when validation was unchecked. The Triager incident recorded `agent_session_create_failed` after the second human answer. Its cleanup could repeatedly hit `operational_evidence_limit` with verified clean source.

## Root Cause

Operational finalization used the broad outcome cause for the terminal event even when the execution path supplied a specific reason. The terminal cause reader used the structured field and ignored the older event's reason. The result card rendered every absent terminal result as a validation failure. Cleanup independently archived the complete primary checkout based on failed status, including detached checkouts that matched a fresh protected baseline. Base64 expansion alone pushes a typical checkout over the archive limit once its source exceeds 288 MiB.

## Solution

Persist the specific execution cause on terminal events and log its normalized value. Log the rejected create command's ID and typed error code with its Run and turn. Recover registered reason tokens and typed error codes from older operational events with a generic structured cause. Explain unchecked execution failures and diagnostic archive limits in the result card. A fresh clean verdict lets a failed detached checkout release through the existing owner and lease. Changed and indeterminate checkouts still require evidence preservation before removal.

## Prevention

Exercise an unchecked execution failure through terminalization and rendering. Verify that the original event reason remains readable after projection and that unrecognized historical reason strings stay out of the UI. Compare clean and changed detached checkouts through the same failed-run cleanup path.
