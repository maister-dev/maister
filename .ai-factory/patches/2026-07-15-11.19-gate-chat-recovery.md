# Recover expired gate-chat turns after a web-process restart

**Date:** 2026-07-15 11:19
**Files:** `web/lib/services/gate-chat.ts`, `web/lib/reconcile.ts`,
`web/lib/db/schema.ts`, migration `0102_perfect_sumo.sql`, review contracts and
integration coverage
**Severity:** high

## Problem

The in-process gate-chat lease timer could be lost when the web process
restarted. Its durable `pending` turn then continued fencing rework forever,
even though no handler remained to cancel the ACP prompt and restore the L3
workspace baseline.

## Root Cause

Lease expiry was owned only by the live request handler. The durable
coordinator state had no reconcile-time recovery owner, and the database
constraint allowed a `completed` turn without the agent message or completion
timestamp required by its lifecycle.

## Solution

Reconciliation now claims expired turns using a durable recovery lease, cancels
the live supervisor prompt, restores the checkpoint baseline, and only then
terminalizes the turn as `aborted`. A failed recovery remains fenced for a
later retry. Migration `0102` adds the terminal-state invariant: `completed`
requires the agent message and completion timestamp; `failed` and `aborted`
require an error and completion timestamp and cannot retain an agent message.
The review count source, manifest-independent review-gate detection, test
mocks, diff wrapping, and ADR-138 references were aligned with that lifecycle.

## Prevention

Any durable interaction that is normally settled by an in-memory callback must
have a reconciliation owner that can complete or safely retain its fence after
a process restart. A batch must acquire each recovery lease at processing time,
not at scan time, so later rows retain their full exclusive window. Keep
numbered artifacts additive: Plan Review remains ADR-137/migration `0100`,
Flow Review remains ADR-138/migration `0101`, and cross-cutting constraints
receive the next migration number.
