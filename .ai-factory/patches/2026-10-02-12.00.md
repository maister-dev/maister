## Causes and correction

- The agent interruption trigger intercepted any update while a run was
  Review, including tail-event sequence allocation before rework admission.
  Limit it to actual status transitions. Existing rework coverage now proves
  metadata and same-status writes pass, then verifies the intended admitted
  turn and terminal host receipt before recovery.
- P4 snapshots an incarnation that prompt admission may create before its
  asynchronous activation. Hold that exact successor's created event, prove
  its pre-projection state, release and await activation before the unchanged
  stale-ACK equality and write audit. This isolates the old handler's effects.
- D-A8's deliberately short permission cap could fire a second time during
  restarted Next startup. Hold only the successor prompt until web readiness
  and durable admission, check its command identity, then release. Preserve
  the initial cap, two-tick recovery and stored-answer assertions. Disposition
  on failure follows the existing owned-process-killed contract.
- P4's cleanup error was its unreleased prompt after the equality failure.
  S1 launch/D2b cleanup-only failures remain unlocalized: the failed job did
  not preserve their nested exceptions. Log cleanup stage and nested errors,
  and bounded safe identities on group-signal refusal. Keep every failure and
  ownership check; do not hide it with retries or widen signal authority.
