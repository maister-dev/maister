## Root causes and repair

- A client closing during an awaited SSE write raised a transport error into
  the proxy's failure list. Normalize only known disconnect codes at that write,
  and only an actually aborted upload plus `ECONNRESET` at request ingestion.
  Keep structured metadata. Remove the unconditional destroyed-response catch
  guard: parser/frame errors must still be recorded after cancellation.
- The macOS reader revalidated process start identity but published parent,
  group and status from its first kernel read. Publish the second read after
  checking PID, UID and start identity. Full environment scans now preserve
  bounded numeric syscall diagnostics, without argv/environment bytes.
- Disposable workers could be the last client of shared Ryuk. Installed
  Testcontainers10.28 retains an unreferenced socket in its public reaper object;
  installed Ryuk0.11 retires after its reconnect grace. Acquire that public
  object in the owning runner before workers, retain it through invocation
  cleanup and release it by actual process exit. Disabled/unavailable reaping
  fails before worker spawn. Bound the caller with the existing 30-second
  Docker deadline and owner cancellation; reject late runtime discovery before
  it starts reaper acquisition. Supervisor-only execution remains Docker-free.
  Never stop/delete shared Ryuk as lane cleanup. The direct control now mints
  its private invocation before spawn, so a missing completion message cannot
  hide the capability needed to reclaim exact inner resources after runner death.

The last two defects are source-supported and reproduced locally; the hosted
retirement interleaving and zombie omission are not claimed as reproduced.
The native control interposes only scheduling around real kernel reads and
uses actual tagged children, `setpgid`, `SIGSTOP` and exit. Darwin25.6 omits
the unreaped dead child rather than providing a usable zombie row; fresh
status/group and environment-failure diagnostics are the proven local scope.
Exact environment ownership, UID/start checks, foreign-process refusal,
watchdogs and terminal containment failures remain intact.
Final review refined only fixture scheduling: the actual `proc_listpids` result
places its target first so unrelated uninspectable PIDs cannot exhaust the
16-diagnostic limit before its receipt. It preserves every real PID and field.
The fixture compiled with strict warnings before the final full lane reached
O-identity; final fixture SHA256 is
`0a96cf9ebb2328701cca4e6fd97befed63586247c410d1b9164c480cf6491a36`.

## Prevention

Classify transport cancellation at the socket boundary, while parser and
barrier errors remain fail-fast. Revalidate mutable kernel fields as well as
identity. Retain shared infrastructure through the lifetime of its actual
owner, and test worker death against real resources on a quiet daemon before
using full-lane green as evidence. Adversarial review found and repaired the
post-disconnect parser suppression, one-shot child registration race, unnecessary
supervisor Docker prerequisite, unbounded acquisition and missing pre-spawn
private capability. Final review also made the native diagnostic control's real
PID scheduling independent of unrelated omissions.
