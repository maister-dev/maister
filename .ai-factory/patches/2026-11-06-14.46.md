## Problem and cause

The external qualification summary declared only modelStarted=false, although
its service serializes the same neutral null/null/null failure projection as the
Web API. Both schemas also permitted neutral model uncertainty alongside
non-neutral preparation details, contrary to the documented projection.
Preparation descriptions and plan acceptance copy implied no earlier model
execution even though continuation preparation can fail after a previous session.
The plan additionally named checkout rather than the browser fault's proven
baseline Git stage.

## Resolution

Align the external source-failure shape and predicates with Web. Constrain the
neutral combination in both schemas; preserve the distinct proven-false case.
Describe preparation without inventing global execution history, and keep the
plan's diagnostic expectations aligned with actual stage and proof. No runtime,
DTO, database or historical receipt is changed.

## Evidence and prevention

The existing contract validator reproduced rejection of the actual neutral DTO
and acceptance of contradictory neutral details before the schema corrections.
Its complete twelve-case owner passes afterward. One grouped regression covers
neutral/proven values, true/private-field/impossible-detail refusals and exact
Web/external parity. Logs are /private/tmp/maister-external-source-failure-red.log,
/private/tmp/maister-source-failure-coupling-red.log and
/private/tmp/maister-source-failure-contract-final-green.log.

Trace all adapters of a shared projection before changing nullability. Reconcile
normative prose and acceptance bullets with their field schemas; preserving a
cause from one preparation does not prove that a Run never executed a model.
