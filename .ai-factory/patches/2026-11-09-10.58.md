# Confirm descendant retirement within the session shutdown grace

**Date:** 2026-11-09 10:58 UTC
**Files:** supervisor/src/execution-fence.ts; supervisor/src/__tests__/session-process-ownership.integration.test.ts; supervisor/test/fixtures/term-delayed-descendant-acp.mjs; web/e2e/_seed/native-qualification-diagnostics.ts
**Severity:** high

## Problem

The real RAH coordinator launched its children, then failed while parking because session deletion returned session_delete_exit_unconfirmed. A direct probe of the configured adapter confirmed DELETE503 after 21ms: the leader and durable terminal event were complete, but a descendant remained alive. The recorded group was still active 250ms later.

## Root Cause

Shutdown correctly waited for leader exit and output persistence, then checked process-group retirement exactly once. Leader exit does not prove that every descendant has finished. The handler therefore refused immediately instead of allowing descendants to use the remaining configured grace. Qualification diagnostics also omitted the typed termination stage.

## Solution

Each TERM/KILL phase shares a monotonic grace budget across leader/output confirmation and subsequent positive process-group observations. Existing ownership, OS-boot and PID-namespace checks remain; no group signal is permitted after its leader exits. Surviving descendants or unavailable inspection retain ownership and cannot establish success. The existing inspector has a separate five-second probe timeout, so the phase grace is not a strict total HTTP deadline. The diagnostic projection retains only the two known termination stages, excluding arbitrary error text.

## Prevention

Use a real delayed-TERM descendant alongside the indefinite-survivor and incomplete-output negative controls. Verify actual group retirement, released ownership, durable command receipts and idempotent replay. Probe the actual configured adapter: a fake single process cannot establish retirement behavior for its subprocess tree. Never acknowledge from leader exit alone or weaken process identity to force success.

## Verification

The new real-process regression is RED 503 versus 204 and then GREEN. Both owning suites pass 30/30; the full supervisor integration lane passes 388/40 and its unit lane passes 523/51. The configured adapter returns 204 in 640ms with positive group retirement at response and 250ms later; its probe cleanup has no surviving owned processes. Independent adversarial review finds no actionable defect. The real-Postgres diagnostic smoke covers known-stage projection, concealment of an arbitrary stage and existing secret/output bounds. Primary-checkout evidence is retained separately.
