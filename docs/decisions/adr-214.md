# ADR-214: Operational preparation and undispatched cancellation

**Date:** 2026-11-20

**Status:** Proposed

## Context

An operational continuation can arrive with an admitted resume turn and zero
dispatch evidence in its current assignment. A predecessor may still have an
unreconciled historical incarnation. A shared repository ref or tracking
configuration update can trigger a false preparation refusal in a linked checkout.

## Decision

- Scope the ephemeral source witness to the checkout and execution-relevant
  configuration. Read historical v1 witnesses for inspection. Require a v2
  ephemeral witness for a new continuation.
- Record definitive refusal before releasing preparation ownership. Persist a
  bounded retry episode for transient failures. Respect true epoch fencing
  at every durable write. Bind a post-create refusal to the original turn and
  incarnation, and block create and prompt admission while it settles.
- Allow the current owner to prove negative dispatch under the admission
  locks. An authorized Stop can abandon that assignment immediately.
  Dispose the checkout only after historical lifecycle and host retirement
  are proved. Exact successful checkpoint receipts and confirmed exits can
  repair historical active or lost incarnations without restoring their bindings.
- Apply retained prompt receipts only to their exact owner. Bound service
  retry and retain a typed incident at exhaustion.

## Consequences

The scheduler slot is released by the terminal Run transaction. The checkout
can remain retained while historical evidence is incomplete. Legacy ephemeral
continuations whose integrity cannot be proved can be explicitly relaunched
as fresh Runs. Existing result contracts and epoch fences continue to apply.
When settlement exhausts its budget, the Run exposes its blocker and incident.
An authorized operator can recheck the current observation once without resetting
the budget or reopening preparation.
