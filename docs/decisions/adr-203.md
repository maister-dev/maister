# ADR-203: Durable specialist assignments and company coordinator Flows

[Back to ADR index](../decisions.md#adr-203-durable-specialist-assignments-and-company-coordinator-flows)

**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Status:** Accepted

**Context:** Agent roles, immutable scheduled requests, operational completion, RAH, child results and human work assignments already exist. Individual runs do not preserve one business responsibility through clarification, coordinator replacement and uncertain external effects. Existing ordinary agent/Flow launch and trigger storage require projects.

**Decision:** Introduce `specialist_assignments` as durable business work linked to existing runs/results, not a replacement attempt ledger. Keep human `assignments` and execution-host `execution_assignments` distinct. Extend installed/pinned package bindings to the explicit installation company owner. Company coordination uses a workspace-none, result-only declaration in the existing graph engine, with installed `flow_revisions` and no project/carrier task. Use the existing scheduler, ACP runtime, HITL, outbox, delegation admission and collection. Version assignment input and working context, fence execution ownership, and require explicit exact-result/evidence acceptance.

The contract is **Designed**. [Runtime](../system-analytics/specialist-runtime.md), [assignments](../system-analytics/specialist-assignments.md) and [cooperation](../system-analytics/specialist-cooperation.md) own behavior.

**Consequences:** Company scopes require complete token, trigger, actor/HITL, run materialization, authorization and read-model changes. Project repository work retains task qualification and workspace rules. Historical inputs/policy snapshots remain inspectable while current permissions govern actions. Retry/rework/replacement cannot reset total assignment budgets or erase completed work. External operations remain independently reconcilable after a run stops.

**Alternatives Considered:** Repurposing human/host assignments conflates different lifecycles. Fake projects violate repository ownership. A second scheduler/orchestrator duplicates mature recovery and accounting. Durable unbounded native subagents bypass the governed admission/collection seam.

**Amendments:**

- 2026-11-09: Company bindings, standalone/result-only graph runtime, scoped intake, context, clarification and result-availability delivery are implemented. Native work dispatch/acceptance, replacement-owner recovery and stand qualification remain Designed; [specialist runtime](../system-analytics/specialist-runtime.md) and [cooperation](../system-analytics/specialist-cooperation.md) distinguish their exact boundaries.
