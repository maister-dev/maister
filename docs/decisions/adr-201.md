# ADR-201: Fenced agent workspace preparation

[Back to ADR index](../decisions.md#adr-201-fenced-agent-workspace-preparation)

**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Status:** Accepted

**Context:** Initial agent launch and continuation can enter checkout preparation before session/turn ownership exists. An absent baseline does not establish that the same run's checkout is unused. Late command fencing cannot undo earlier filesystem mutations, and cleanup cannot see an unrecorded preparer.

**Decision:** Record preparation ownership before filesystem mutation, bind it to exact assignment/epoch/resource/operation, and serialize physical preparation and disposal with a stable cross-process resource fence. Validate and reuse owned resources rather than removing them because no baseline exists. Lease expiry invalidates publication, not physical activity; takeover requires exclusion/quiescence covering surviving child operations. Publish a real baseline and hand off durably to existing session/create/prompt ownership. Terminal cleanup follows the committed terminal transition.

The contract is **Designed**. [Agent preparation](../system-analytics/agent-preparation.md) owns the phase/lease/crash-window rules. A local checkout-removal mechanism was reproduced; the historical MAI-27 competing operation owners and lost inner exception remain unproven.

**Consequences:** Preparation becomes visible to cancellation, disposition and restart recovery. The resource mutex lives outside removable trees and is stable across epochs. Safe original diagnostic cause is retained separately from missing-baseline/publication/cleanup consequences. The process-child lifetime invariant must be proven before physical takeover is enabled.

**Alternatives Considered:** A process-local mutex, delayed redrive, an unchecked expired lease, unconditional remove/add, ignoring missing baseline, or weakening integrity gates do not protect a live checkout across processes and restarts.
